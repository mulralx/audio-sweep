Audio sweep test

Plays a test signal through the speaker and records it with the microphone at the same time, then reports how clean the recording is. Nothing is uploaded; everything stays in this page.

  1. Set the volume to maximum and put the phone flat on a table, in a quiet room.
  2. Tap a test button and allow the microphone (and motion sensors if asked).
  3. Listen: does the sound come from the main speaker or from the earpiece?

Ready.

Recorded level along the sweep (200 Hz to 8 kHz), then the high chirp (11 to 20 kHz). The dashed line is the background noise.
